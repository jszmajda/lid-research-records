Phase 2 is drafted. 2Q is recorded in the previews LLD — no new interface, queue, or budget change, so the decision stayed in the previews segment; I recorded it as rows in that LLD's Decisions & Alternatives table (policy choice, no-promote-on-A1in-hit, no ghost record for Am evictions) rather than opening a decision doc, since nothing outside previews is sized around it. One HLD edit: the Architecture bullet said "Its eviction policy remains open," which is now stale — it reads "including its replacement policy and all replacement bookkeeping."

**What the LLD now says** (`docs/intent/previews/previews-design.md § Replacement policy: 2Q`): A1in, a FIFO of resident first-use sheets on a byte share; A1out, a keys-only ghost FIFO on a count bound; Am, an LRU of proven-reuse sheets. First admission goes to A1in; a completion whose key is in A1out goes to Am's MRU end. A hit in A1in serves bytes and does not move the sheet; a hit in Am moves it to MRU. Eviction takes A1in's tail into A1out when A1in is over share, otherwise Am's LRU end with the key discarded. Starting parameters: A1in 3 GiB / Am 9 GiB, A1out 3,000 keys. Structures rebuild empty on restart.

**The edge-case probe** (delegated to a reader given only the previews docs) came back with 20 gaps. Ranked triage — my proposed resolution for each, so you can confirm or correct rather than re-derive:

1. **Is the 3/9 split hard or a remainder?** The one I most want your call on. Hard partition wastes up to 3 GiB when A1in is empty; "Am takes 12 GiB minus A1in's actual use" never wastes budget but lets Am squeeze A1in's scan buffer down. I lean hard partition — the fixed buffer is what makes the scan resistance predictable — but it does mean a comparison-only week runs Am at 9 GiB, not 12.
2. **Eviction doesn't necessarily free the destination queue.** Under a hard partition, evicting A1in to admit a promoted sheet into Am doesn't help. Fix: evict from the destination queue until the incoming sheet fits its share, projected occupancy including the new sheet.
3. **A1out's count bound can drop a key whose render is in flight**, contradicting the stated "ghost record survives the following render job." Fix: exempt keys with a pending job from A1out eviction, making the bound soft by at most four keys.
4. **Completion for a key already resident** (late job after a retry, or a race with restart re-admission) isn't covered. Fix: overwrite in place, no promotion, no second byte charge.
5. **Disjointness is unstated** — "absent from both queues" is ambiguous. Fix: state the invariant that a key is in at most one of A1in, A1out, Am, and name the pair explicitly.
6. **Surviving cache files on restart are outside all three structures**, so their bytes aren't counted — real footprint can exceed 12 GiB, and a never-requested file leaks. Fix: scan files at start and count their bytes, re-admitting to A1in on first request.
7. **A sheet bigger than its destination's share** empties the queue and still doesn't fit. Fix: admit it anyway and let the share go over transiently; at 2 MiB typical this is theoretical.
8. **A1in-hit recording isn't normatively stated** — an implementer may add a touch bit "harmlessly" and change eviction order. Fix: say explicitly that an A1in hit records nothing.

Five findings are pre-existing gaps this change doesn't touch — the response shape when a shared job fails and a client is polling (PREVIEW-005 says nothing), re-enqueue stampede after failure, unbounded render-queue depth with no shed-load outcome, job-ticket shape and lifetime, and whether temp render output counts against the 12 GiB. I'd defer those as Open Questions rather than fold them into this change.

Say "go" to apply 1–8 as I've described (or give me numbers with corrections), and tell me whether to defer that last group. Then I'll move to Phase 3 — PREVIEW-004 currently says only "evict before admission," so 2Q's admission, promotion, and ghost-record behaviors need new spec lines under it.
