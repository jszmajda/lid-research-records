Wrote the decision doc at `docs/intent/record-store/decisions/concurrent-visit-edits.md` and pointed the LLD's Decisions & Alternatives row at it.

It lays out both options against the gates and criteria (no-discard, inspectable-export, unreviewed-combination risk, review workload, maintenance burden, export readiness, submission size), and the Selection section spells out why field-aware merge loses even on its own strongest claim: it either auto-combines without review — failing the inspectable-export gate whenever the combined fields were actually scientifically linked — or routes every combination through manual approval, at which point it no longer beats whole-visit revisions on review workload either. It also names what tablet-sync and review-export inherit: tablet-sync retains base revision IDs and must show a worker their submission as pending-alongside-a-competitor rather than merged or silently superseded; review-export needs the revision-comparison view and must hold any visit with unresolved competing revisions out of the CSV entirely, with no partial/auto-approved export path.

One thing from last time is still open and this didn't settle it: should a submission that arrives while a visit already has an unresolved competing revision, but whose base revision matches current, still be forced into "competing" (keeping the coordinator's comparison set stable for that review) — or should it fast-path to current as usual?
