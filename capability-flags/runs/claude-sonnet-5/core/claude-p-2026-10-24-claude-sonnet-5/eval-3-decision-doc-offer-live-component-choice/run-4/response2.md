I wrote up the decision doc at `docs/intent/record-store/decisions/concurrency-reconciliation-policy.md`, and pointed the LLD's Decisions row at it instead of carrying the full rationale inline.

The doc lays out: the two major criteria that actually conflict (review workload/export-readiness favor field-aware merge; scientific-relationship risk/inspectability favors whole-visit revisions), why whole-visit wins despite losing on workload, what it forecloses (no server-side field combination, ever), and the two concrete things it obligates downstream — tablet-sync must track and submit base revision IDs and make a worker's "your edit is now contested" state legible; review-export must get a whole-revision comparison view and must hold any contested visit out of the Friday export regardless of which fields actually conflict. It also names what would have to be true to revisit field-aware merge later (a trustworthy way to declare which fields are scientifically coupled, or evidence the inspectability risk is smaller in practice than the review cost).
