**F2 — High. The HLD's first tenet is "A submitted job is a promise: members should be able to upload and walk away," and the overnight-print goal assumes nobody is present to notice a lost job or resubmit it. Under *tenet cascade* — design nodes should lean the way the HLD's tenets and goals say to lean — this is the clear conflict case: a described operating condition (a restart) defeats a stated promise, and nothing on the record acknowledges the trade. The cost of leaving it: the project's headline promise silently doesn't hold across the most ordinary failure mode on that hardware, and a cold reader implementing from these docs has no cue that a durability decision is even in play.
