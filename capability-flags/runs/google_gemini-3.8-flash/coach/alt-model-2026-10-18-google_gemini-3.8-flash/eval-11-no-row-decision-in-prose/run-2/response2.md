**F2 — High. In-memory queue storage drops undispatched jobs on mini-PC restarts, conflicting with HLD tenets and goals.** In `docs/intent/job-queue/job-queue-design.md` (§ Architecture), the queue of accepted jobs is held entirely in process memory, with the design noting that jobs accepted but not yet dispatched before a restart are lost and require member resubmission. However, the LLD's context explicitly notes that the mini-PC takes unattended OS updates and occasional power blips, which directly contradicts the HLD's core promise (*"A submitted job is a promise: members should be able to upload and walk away"*) and primary goal (*"Evening upload, overnight print, morning pickup — with no member present"*). Under *tenet cascade* — leaf design mechanisms must lean the way the HLD's tenets and goals dictate under described operating conditions — an unaligned mechanism creates an architectural contradiction that downstream implementations cannot resolve cleanly. Leaving this unaddressed means an unattended overnight reboot will silently break the makerspace's central user promise; resolving it requires choosing between aligning the mechanism with the tenet, recording an explicit exception, or adjusting the tenet.

**F3 — Medium. The choice of ephemeral in-memory queue storage is unrecorded in Decisions & Alternatives.** While `docs/intent/job-queue/job-queue-design.md` records decisions for scheduling order, job identifiers, and upload limits, the choice to keep accepted jobs purely in memory rather than persisting them lives only as an assertion in body prose. Under *docs carry current intent, written to be read cold* — load-bearing choices that a cold reader would question or seek to reverse require captured rationale and accepted costs — leaving this architectural choice out of the table obscures whether ephemeral storage was a deliberate trade-off or an oversight, especially given that `printer-registry` chose SQLite persistence and G-code files are already stored on disk. Future sessions cannot verify what alternatives were weighed or what operational costs were deemed acceptable without that reasoning on record.
