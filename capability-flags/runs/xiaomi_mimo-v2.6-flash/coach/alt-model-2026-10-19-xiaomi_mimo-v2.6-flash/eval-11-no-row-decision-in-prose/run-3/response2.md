**F2 — High. The in-memory queue defeats the "promise" tenet, with no exception on record.** `docs/intent/job-queue/job-queue-design.md:14` states that the queue is "held entirely in process memory" and that "jobs that were accepted but not yet dispatched before a restart are gone, and members resubmit them." The HLD's first tenet is "A submitted job is a promise: members should be able to upload and walk away" (`docs/high-level-design.md:20`), and its first goal is "evening upload, overnight print, morning pickup — with no member present." A power blip at 2 a.m. means the member *is* present, or their job doesn't print. Under *tenet cascade* — design nodes should lean the way the HLD's tenets say to lean, and any exception should be recorded naming the tenet traded against — this is a direct conflict with no Decisions row or decision doc explaining it.
