Under *tenets capture forward-looking preference* — tenets state which way leaf designs should lean when trade-offs arise — and *coherence is adjacency* — each level of the arrow agrees with the level above it — this mechanism pulls directly against `docs/high-level-design.md:20-26`, which establishes the tenet *"A submitted job is a promise: members should be able to upload and walk away"* and the goal *"Evening upload, overnight print, morning pickup — with no member present."* If a member uploads a job at 9 PM and walks away, an overnight unattended OS reboot silently drops their queued job before a printer frees up, leaving them with no print in the morning and no way to have resubmitted while asleep; leaving this unaddressed means downstream agents writing specs, tests, and code have to guess whether the HLD's promise or the LLD's ephemeral memory model is authoritative.
