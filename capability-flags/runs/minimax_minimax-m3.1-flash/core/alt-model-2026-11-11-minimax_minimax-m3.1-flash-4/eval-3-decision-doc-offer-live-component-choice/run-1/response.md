**Phase 2 — record-store LLD rewritten** (`docs/intent/record-store/record-store-design.md`), whole-visit revisions throughout:

- **Revision model** — every revision stores the complete submitted visit payload, its author, receipt time, per-visit sequence number, and the base revision it was made from. Ancestry is recorded as submitted and never rewritten.
- **Acceptance rules** — base == head → new head; base == head while a review group is open → joins the group; base is any other known revision → competing revision, head frozen; unknown/mismatched base or invalid payload → rejected with a stable reason (invalid visit state / invalid base revision / invalid payload / idempotency key reused).
- **Unresolved review work** — one item per visit: head revision, competing revision IDs, opened time. Payloads come from submission history, not copied into the item.
- **Resolution** — coordinator role selects one competing revision; it becomes the head, the displaced head becomes *superseded*, losers become *resolved without selection*, all contributing revision IDs and the coordinator's identity are retained. Outcomes are distinguished: not authorized / nothing to resolve / invalid selection / resolved.
- **Export eligibility** — no unresolved review work, queried over an observation-date range. Every conflict-free visit is exportable without a coordinator's per-visit sign-off (6,000 visits vs. two coordinators).
- **Downstream contract** — submission envelope, submission outcome, current-state response, submission history, resolution operation, export-eligible query: the six shapes tablet-sync and review-export will size against.
