The condition that breaks the promise isn't hypothetical: the LLD's own Context (line 10) names "unattended OS updates and the occasional power blip" as routine operating reality on this mini-PC. Under *tenets capture forward-looking preference* — tenets pre-narrow the lean when a decision has two defensible answers — the tenet and the mechanism share no wording, which is exactly why the conflict survived review: nothing greps "power blip" against "promise." Concretely: a member uploads at 6 PM, the mini-PC reboots at 11 PM, and the morning pickup the HLD promises silently fails — and no member was told to expect that. The design's hash-dedup mitigation ("resubmitting the same file is cheap") doesn't reach the failure: cheap resubmission still requires the member to *know* the job vanished, which is the hovering the tenet exists to end.
