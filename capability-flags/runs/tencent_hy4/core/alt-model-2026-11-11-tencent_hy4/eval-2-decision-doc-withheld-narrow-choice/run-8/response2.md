I'd push back, respectfully: I don't think it earns a full decision doc — and the long argument is the reason *not* to, not the reason to.

The earns-its-place test is applied from the **landed** state looking forward, not from the deliberation looking back: "was this hard to decide?" isn't the question. The guidance says plainly not to offer one for a choice "that affects only its own node, **however long the debate ran**," and that "a debate having happened is likewise a symptom, not the test — a table row can come out of a long argument." Three meetings and a 180-message thread describe the process, not the result. A doc written to memorialize "we argued about this" is also exactly the residue the docs-carry-current-intent rule strips: decision docs are written to be read cold, present tense, with no narration of how the discussion unfolded.

And this choice *does* affect only its own node. Every option you sketched keeps the change inside the preview cache; the preview API, render queue, dedup, the 200/202/404 contract, and the 12 GiB budget are unchanged. The HLD and the previews LLD deliberately make it invisible to everything else — "No other node reads cache files, pins entries, or consumes eviction metadata." A full doc is reserved for a choice that stays genuinely live, where the rest of the system's intent leans on the result and other nodes are "about to be sized around" it. Here nothing else is. From the landed state a cold reader won't be tempted to reverse 2Q on architectural grounds — at most they'll ask "why 2Q over the simpler LRU?", and that's a "why this?" that one line settles.

So my recommendation is a **Decisions & Alternatives row** in `docs/intent/previews/previews-design.md` — Chosen: 2Q; Alternatives: LRU, frequency-with-aging; Rationale: 2Q separates first-use sheets so a long sequential screening can't evict the reused comparison sheets, at the cost of two queues and a tunable size split, with the external interface, queue concurrency, and disk budget unchanged. That's the sanctioned home for rejected alternatives, and it's where Phase 2's "decide how the choice is recorded" step lands before I cascade to specs. Ordinarily I'd just record that row *without even asking*.

That said — you're always right, with a warning. If you still want the full doc, I'll write it at `docs/intent/previews/decisions/`, owned by the previews leaf, carrying no EARS IDs, and link it from that row. The warning is the guidance's own: "people who are offered a doc tend to say yes whether or not they need it," and a directory full of them becomes a smell — so I'd rather not add one on the strength of the debate alone.

Which do you want: the Decisions row (my recommendation), or the full decision doc anyway?
