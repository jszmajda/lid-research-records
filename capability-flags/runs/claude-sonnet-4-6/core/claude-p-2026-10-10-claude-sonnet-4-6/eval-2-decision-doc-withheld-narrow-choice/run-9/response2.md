The skill has a specific test for this, and the answer is no — but the reason is worth understanding, because it's not about how hard the decision was.

The test is applied from the **landed state**: would a cold reader question the choice or be tempted to reverse it? And there's an explicit carveout: *do not write a decision doc for a choice that affects only its own node, however long the debate ran.* The 2Q decision lives entirely inside the preview worker's private cache — the HLD even calls this out as a tenet ("keep cache paths and replacement bookkeeping private to the preview worker"). Nothing outside the previews segment is sized around this choice.

The length of the debate is real, but it's deliberation cost, not landing weight. A decision doc memorializes choices a cold reader would re-litigate *from the result alone*. A cold reader landing on a contact-sheet preview cache using 2Q with a 20% Q1 split might say "why not LRU?" — and the Decisions & Alternatives table row answers that in one line. That's exactly what the row is for.

Writing a full doc here would be recording the *effort* of deciding, not the *value* of the record. The skill calls that residue.

The table row already does the job. I'd leave it there.
