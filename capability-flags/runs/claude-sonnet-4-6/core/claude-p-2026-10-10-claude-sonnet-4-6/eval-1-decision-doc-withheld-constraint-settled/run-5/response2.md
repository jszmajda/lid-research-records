The skill explicitly carves out this case: "Do not offer one for a choice... that an inherited constraint settles." The deliberation felt close because the tenet's implication wasn't immediately obvious, but that's a deliberation cost, not a landing cost. The doc would end up summarizing the tenet back at the reader, which isn't load-bearing new content.
