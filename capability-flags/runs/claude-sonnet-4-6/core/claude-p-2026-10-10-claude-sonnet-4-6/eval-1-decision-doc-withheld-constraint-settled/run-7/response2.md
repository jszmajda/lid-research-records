Overkill. The decision doc test is the landed state: *would a cold reader find the choice non-obvious and be tempted to reverse it?*

A cold reader who reaches the Decisions row and then checks the HLD tenet — "All serial-number lookup SHALL complete using files on the kiosk; no runtime network access is permitted" — would see that the tenet rules out both alternatives on its own. The choice doesn't stay live without the competing options in front of them; the tenet settles it.

The skill's exact carveout covers this: don't write a doc for "a choice an inherited constraint settles… however long the debate ran." The debate duration doesn't factor in — only whether a cold reader would re-litigate. Here they wouldn't. The Decisions row is the right home.
