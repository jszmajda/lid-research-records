A separate decision doc would be overkill.

From the landed system’s perspective, the HLD’s offline-operation and USB-only update constraints largely eliminate the network-backed alternatives. A cold reader may still ask why SQLite was selected, but the existing Decisions & Alternatives row answers that adequately.

The amount of deliberation does not itself justify a decision doc; one would become worthwhile only if network-backed operation remained a credible future direction or the choice depended on several competing criteria that maintainers might reasonably re-litigate.
