A **Decisions & Alternatives row is enough here**.

The test is whether a future reader would need the full tradeoff analysis to understand or reconsider the choice—not how difficult it felt to decide. The HLD already requires kiosk-local lookup and prohibits runtime network access, which rules out the two service options as described. SQLite’s rationale fits in one row, and its consequences stay within the registry; the certificate interface remains unchanged.

A standalone decision doc would earn its place if network-backed lookup became viable and the choice remained a genuine tradeoff shaping multiple components. Under the current constraints, it would be overkill.
