The test is whether a cold reader of the finished design would question the choice or be tempted to reverse it, not whether it was hard to decide. Here the HLD tenets settle it. Kiosks have no network route, and all lookup must complete from files on the kiosk. That rules out the HTTPS directory and the managed search service before cost or convenience comes into it. A reader who sees the row, with its "both alternatives need runtime network access" rationale, has what they need.

That your back-and-forth was real doesn't change this. Debate length isn't the criterion, and the options only looked close because the earlier sketch weighed them on maintenance and cost without applying the tenets.

A full decision doc is for choices that stay live. That would be credible options traded against several criteria, with other nodes about to be sized around the result. Neither holds here, and the certificates node is unaffected by the choice.
